- i look for various designs online, for homelabs and small racks for homes on reddit, and after looking them i choose to make a all in one small rack with modular design so i can add or remove layers according to me and my needs.
- started off with a base of 160x160mm base as we have common prints of print size 180x180 so its super safe and pretty small too.
